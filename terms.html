What this is

Creator Gacha is a free, non-commercial fan project and portfolio piece. It turns public YouTube channel statistics into collectible trading cards: subscriber count sets a card's rarity, view count its attack, video count its defence.

It is unofficial. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or connected to YouTube, Google, or any creator whose channel appears on a card. Channel data comes from the YouTube Data API. All trademarks belong to their owners, and are used here descriptively — to say what the data is — not to suggest any relationship.

There is no money in the game

This is a hard rule, not a current state of affairs:

  • no purchases, no currency, no paid pulls, no premium cards, no perks;
  • no advertising;
  • no trading, sale or transfer of cards.

There is a single "Buy me a coffee" link in the footer. It buys the author a coffee and nothing else — it unlocks no cards, no odds, no features, and it never will. Cards are drawn with fixed published odds and no money can change them, which keeps this a toy rather than a game of chance.

If you are a creator on a card

You can have your card removed, and you do not have to explain why. Write to ashish.barthwal.cs@gmail.com.

  • You will be removed from every published set within 7 days.
  • No proof of identity is asked for. An unfounded request costs one card; a verification gauntlet would cost the good faith this promise exists to show.
  • The removal is permanent. Your channel id is added to a denylist that both evicts it and blocks it from every future sourcing run, so it cannot reappear by accident.

Cards carry no exported images and no download button, deliberately: an exported copy would outlive a removal, and a promise that cannot be performed is not worth making.

Using it

Play it. Don't attempt to break, overload or misuse the site or the APIs behind it, and don't use it to harass anyone. Nothing is asked of you to play — no account, no API key, no setup.

Accuracy, availability, and the usual disclaimer

Card statistics are a snapshot, refreshed on a cadence and never published more than 30 days old. They will not match a channel's live numbers, and stats are not a judgement about any creator. The site is provided "as is", with no warranty of any kind, and may change, break, or be taken down at any time. Your collection is stored only in your own browser — clearing site data deletes it, and there is no backup.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, and your API key never leaves the page. The details are in the privacy policy.
