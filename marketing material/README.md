# Creator Gacha marketing kit

Working material for introducing Creator Gacha on LinkedIn. Keep this folder local until a release is approved; `.gitignore` excludes it.

## Positioning

Creator Gacha is a personal, unofficial fan project that turns public YouTube channel data into collectible cards and an auto-resolved, five-on-five team battler. Subscriber count determines rarity. Channel performance and history shape a card's battle profile. Players collect locally, build a team, and watch the fight play out.

The strongest professional story is the connected product and engineering: a complete browser game, a deterministic rules engine, carefully bounded multiplayer coordination, and a visual system built around the same card identity from pack to battle. Lead with the playful idea; use later posts to unpack how it works.

## Series arc

1. **Temperature check:** Is the creator-card premise interesting? What would people want to see?
2. **The pull:** Show the pack, rarity reveal, and the card collection loop.
3. **The card has a point of view:** Explain how channel data maps to rarity and combat stats; invite discussion about the design.
4. **Build a team:** Show five-card selection, class and element choices, and formation.
5. **Watch the battle:** Short gameplay clip that makes the auto-resolved fight legible, then shows the result.
6. **How it is built:** Vanilla JavaScript, pure deterministic game rules, local-first collection, and a small multiplayer coordination service. The architecture diagrams are planned from two separate briefs: [app and backend](linkedin-app-backend-diagram-brief.md) and [battle system](linkedin-battle-system-diagram-brief.md).
7. **What I learned / what changed:** Respond to feedback with a concrete iteration or design decision.

Do not imply creator endorsement, YouTube affiliation, live-updating statistics, competitive anti-cheat, or a larger current card count without checking current source and deployment evidence.
