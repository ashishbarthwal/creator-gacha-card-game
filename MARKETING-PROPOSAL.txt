CREATOR GACHA — MARKETING PROPOSAL
Working document. Not shipped with the site (build-site.js copies an allowlist).
Written 2026-08-22 against the live build: Core Set, 22,720 cards, snapshot 2026-08-17.
Live at https://creator-gacha.pages.dev


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE ONE LINE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "Pull collectible cards minted from real YouTube channel stats."

That is the shipped tagline and it should stay the shipped tagline. It does the
whole job in nine words: what you do (pull), what you get (cards), and the thing
that makes it not a toy (real stats).

Shorter, for a Reddit title or a tweet:

  "I turned 22,720 YouTubers into gacha cards. Their stats are real."


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IT IS, IN SIXTY SECONDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open a browser. Press a pack. Ten cards fan out with a holo tilt and a rarity
reveal. Every card is a real YouTube channel, and every number on it is derived
from that channel's actual data:

  subscribers        ->  rarity band  (N / R / SR / SSR / UR / RUBY)
  views per video    ->  ATK
  views per sub      ->  DEF
  channel age        ->  HP
  upload cadence     ->  SPD
  subs won per year  ->  MOM

Then you fight with them. Five-card teams, six classes (Titan, Carry, Bulwark,
Assassin, Riser, Balanced), and a six-way element wheel drawn from what the
channel is actually about (Gaming, Tech, Knowledge, Music, Comedy, Lifestyle).

Three ways to start a fight:
  - Quick battle       vs an AI that rolled its own collection on your odds
  - Friend code        send a string, they paste it, you both build blind
  - Find an opponent   random matchmaking against a live stranger

No signup. No download. No API key. No ads. No purchases. The collection saves
to your own browser and nowhere else.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE HOOK — WHY ANYONE WOULD SHARE THIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The marketing asset is not the game loop. It is THE ARGUMENT.

Because the stats are derived and not invented, the cards make claims people
want to fight about. A creator with 200M subscribers can have mediocre DEF. A
small channel with one breakout video is a glass cannon. Somebody's favourite
rates below somebody else's favourite, and the game can show its working.

That is the shareable moment, and it costs nothing to produce: a screenshot of
one card with a surprising number on it. Not "look at this game" — "look at what
this game says about MrBeast."

Three repeatable content shapes fall out of that:
  a) The chase pull.  A UR/RUBY reveal is a three-second video with a payoff.
  b) The verdict.     One card, one hot stat, one line of copy.
  c) The matchup.     Two named creators, one fight, an actual winner.

Secondary hook, for a different room: it is a gacha with NOTHING to buy. That
sentence is genuinely unusual and it lands hard with people tired of the genre.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHO IT IS FOR — FOUR AUDIENCES, FOUR PITCHES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A) YOUTUBE-CULTURE FANS   (the primary audience)
   Pitch: "Your favourite creator is a trading card, and the stats are real."
   They already argue about creators. This gives the argument a scoreboard.
   Surfaces: creator-specific subreddits and Discords, commentary and reaction
   communities, YouTube-culture Twitter.
   Caution: post it as a fan-made thing, because it is one. Anything that smells
   like a brand launch gets removed by mods, and deserves to be.

B) GACHA PLAYERS
   Pitch: "A gacha with no currency, no pity, no paid pulls and no ads. Pull
   until you're bored."
   Surfaces: r/gachagaming and equivalents.
   The rate curve is a selling point to this crowd and should be stated plainly:
   N 55% / R 27% / SR 12% / SSR 5% / UR 0.9% / RUBY 0.1%.

C) THE CREATORS THEMSELVES
   The highest-leverage channel available, and it costs nothing. A creator who
   finds their own card and posts it reaches the exact audience this game is for,
   with an endorsement no ad can buy.
   Pitch: "You're in it. Here's your card."
   Mechanics: a direct link to a specific card would make this trivial to send.
   See section 8 — it does not exist yet, and it is the single highest-value
   missing feature for marketing purposes.
   Caution: this is also the highest-risk channel. See section 6.

D) DEVELOPERS / PORTFOLIO AUDIENCE   (a different product, same URL)
   Pitch: "22,720 cards, zero dependencies, no build step, and every balance
   claim in the repo is a measurement."
   Surfaces: Hacker News, r/webdev, dev Twitter, LinkedIn.
   The story here is the PROCESS, not the game: the decision log, the balance
   tool, the Durable Object migration written up with the bug that forced it.
   This audience converts to job conversations, not players. Do not blend this
   pitch with A/B/C — it is a separate post.

5. WHAT IS ACTUALLY LIVE   (keep the claims honest)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Live and playable right now:
  - 22,720 real creator cards across six rarity bands
  - Pack opening, reveal, persistent collection, search/filter/sort binder
  - Full 5v5 battle engine: classes, elements, formation, crits
  - Quick battle vs AI
  - Cross-device 1v1 by friend code (confirmed working on two real devices)
  - Random matchmaking vs a stranger
  - In-arena field manual explaining every mechanic

On the roadmap, and already stated in the site footer:
  - Tournament runs
  - Difficulty settings
  - Collection milestones
  - Seasonal card sets

Do NOT claim: accounts, saved match history, a ladder, rankings, or a mobile
app. None exist and none are planned.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CONSTRAINTS THAT SHAPE THE MARKETING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These are not caveats to bury. They change what a campaign can look like.

NO REVENUE, EVER. There is no ad slot, no purchase, no tip jar and no sponsor
button, by explicit decision. "Success" cannot be measured in money, and there
is no budget for paid acquisition. Every channel here has to be organic.

LIKENESS EXPOSURE IS THE REAL RISK, AND PROMOTION RAISES IT. Cards currently
carry creators' real profile photos. A quiet live site and an actively promoted
one are different exposure levels, and this is flagged as unresolved in TASKS.md.
Three honest options, one of which has to be picked BEFORE the first real post:
   1. Launch on generated emblems (one flag: AVATAR_SOURCE = 'emblem')
   2. Launch on photos, with the opt-out link and a same-day takedown promise
   3. Take the legal consult first
The opt-out already ships in the footer with a 7-day promise, and the emblem
build is one config line away. That reversibility is the argument for option 2 —
but it is Ash's call, and it is not a technical one.

PEOPLE ONLY, NEVER INSTITUTIONS. Cards are humans and performer-shaped groups.
No companies, labels, studios or brands, on purpose. If a post gets traction,
expect "why isn't [company channel] in it" and answer with the rule — it reads
as care, not as a gap.

NO CHILDREN. 47 minors were removed from the deck deliberately. Worth having the
sentence ready; it is the kind of thing that gets asked in public.

CAPACITY. Quick battle costs nothing and scales indefinitely, so the main game is
immune to a traffic spike. The multiplayer path runs on Cloudflare free-tier
Durable Objects. The OLD KV backend was measured at roughly 150 cross-device
matches/day before the write ceiling bit; the CURRENT backend has not been
re-measured. Do that before any post that could land thousands of people at
once — the failure mode is lobbies that hang rather than a page that errors, and
that reads as "broken game" to a first-time visitor.

THE DISCLAIMER IS NON-NEGOTIABLE. "Fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed
by YouTube or Google." It is in the footer and it belongs in any post that could
be mistaken for official.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT TO MEASURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Cloudflare Pages already records requests, bandwidth and top paths server-side.
Nothing needs deploying and no visitor is touched. That answers "did anyone
come", and it is the only instrumentation this project should have.

Explicitly do NOT switch on Cloudflare Web Analytics or any client-side beacon.
The footer promises "No accounts, no tracking", and a script phoning home on
every pageview is exactly the thing that sentence says is not happening. The
promise is worth more than the funnel data.

So the honest scoreboard is:
  - unique visitors on the day of a post, and the shape of the tail after it
  - whether anyone reaches /api/queue (i.e. did strangers actually pair)
  - replies, screenshots and creator reactions, counted by hand — which is fine
    at this scale


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. THE GAPS THAT WOULD MOST HELP MARKETING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ranked by leverage per hour of work. None are required to launch.

1. A SHAREABLE CARD LINK. Today you can share the site; you cannot share a card.
   "Here is your card" is the entire creator-outreach play in 4C, and it
   currently requires a screenshot. A URL that opens straight to one card — with
   matching og: tags so it unfurls AS that card in Discord and Twitter — turns
   every card in the deck into a distribution surface.

2. og.png IS STILL A MANUAL STEP. The og: and twitter: tags are in place and
   point at /og.png. Confirm the deployed image actually resolves before posting
   anywhere: a scraper that fetches a 404 caches the failure, so the first bad
   share poisons the link for everyone who sees it afterwards.

3. A TEN-SECOND CAPTURE OF A UR PULL. The reveal is the best thing in the product
   and there is currently no video of it. One screen recording is reusable across
   every surface in section 4.

4. A "FIND YOUR CARD" SEARCH ON THE FRONT PAGE. The binder searches what you own.
   A visitor's first question is "is X in it?", and right now the only answer is
   "pull and find out" — the wrong answer for a stranger who has not decided to
   care yet.

9. SUGGESTED SEQUENCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 0 — Decide the avatar question (section 6). Everything else waits on it.
Phase 1 — Verify og.png resolves on the live domain. Capture the UR pull video.
Phase 2 — One dev-audience post (HN / r/webdev). Lowest risk, most forgiving
          crowd, and it stress-tests the site before the players arrive.
Phase 3 — One gacha-audience post, leading with "no monetization, here are the
          real rates".
Phase 4 — Creator outreach, one at a time, small channels first. Only after a
          shareable card link exists — without it, this phase is asking busy
          people to go hunting.

Do not do all four in a week. Each one tells you something the next one needs.
